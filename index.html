Privacy Policy

My Tab Manager PRO — Google Chrome Extension

Last updated: July 21, 2026

100% local · No data collection · No tracking

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how My Tab Manager PRO ("the Extension", "we", "our") handles information when you use it in the Google Chrome browser. The Extension is a tab management tool that helps you organize, group, suspend, and restore browser tabs and sessions.

The short version: My Tab Manager PRO works entirely on your device. It does not collect, process, transmit, store on external servers, share, or sell any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics of any kind.

2. Data We Collect (None)

The Extension does not collect any personal or non-personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information;
  • Browsing history, page content, or website activity beyond what is technically required to manage your tabs locally (see Section 3);
  • Authentication information, passwords, or financial and payment information;
  • Location data, IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage statistics;
  • Health information or any other sensitive category of data.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is ever transmitted to us or to any third party, and nothing is sold, rented, or shared for any purpose, including advertising or profiling.

3. How We Use Permissions

The Extension requests the minimum set of Chrome permissions required for its functionality. All permission-related operations are performed locally in your browser and are never used to gather or transmit data:

  • tabs and tabGroups — required to read the URLs and titles of your open tabs so the Extension can list, close, group, and suspend (freeze) them to free up memory (RAM). This information is processed in memory on your device only.
  • storage — required to save your sessions (lists of URLs) and interface preferences (language, theme color) locally using chrome.storage.local.
  • contextMenus — required to add quick tab-management actions to the browser's right-click context menu.

These permissions are used solely to provide the single, user-facing purpose of the Extension — managing your tabs — in accordance with the Chrome Web Store Program Policies and its User Data / Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage

All data the Extension works with is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage via chrome.storage.local. This includes:

  • Saved sessions (lists of tab URLs you choose to save);
  • Interface settings, such as your preferred language and theme color.

This data never leaves your device, is not synchronized to any external server, and is fully under your control. You can delete it at any time by removing saved sessions within the Extension or by uninstalling the Extension, which permanently erases all of its locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Services (None)

The Extension does not integrate, embed, or communicate with any third-party services. Specifically, it does not use:

  • Google Analytics or any other analytics or telemetry tools;
  • Advertising networks, ad modules, or tracking pixels;
  • Crash-reporting services or remote logging;
  • External APIs, CDNs, or remote code of any kind.

The Extension makes no network requests to external servers during normal operation.

6. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under the age of 13, and — because it collects no data whatsoever — it does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the Extension's functionality or in legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Extension after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
